A team of AI agents that plans a safe running route at the right distance, with stops that are open when you arrive, what to wear, and pacing for every hill.

01 · The problem

Planning a good run is a chore

Who: about 50 million Americans run1, and run clubs grew 59% in 20242.

Today: they repeat the same loop, drag a line on a map app, or improvise. A line on a map doesn't answer:

  • 🛡️Is it safe? Busy roads, unmarked crossings, dark before I'm back?
  • 🥐Will the café I want to end at be open when I get there?
  • ⛰️What should I wear, and how hard should I push on the hills?
  • 🏃For a club: when does each pace group reach the meetup?
7,148pedestrians killed by drivers in the US in 2024, nearly 20% more than in 20163

The cost is time and frustration, plus risk: most people skip the checks and run an unchecked route.

03 · How it works

A team of agents, checked by code

10 AI agents (gpt-6-luna) plus ordinary code. The rule: AI reads, chooses and explains; code calculates every number.

Read left to right in four stages. The safety check can reject a stop and send the Stops Scout back; the writers in stage 4 work at the same time.

05 · Cost to run

About a tenth of a cent per plan

💵$0.001per plan (median, measured on 8 real requests)
⏱️11.2 sper plan, 5 to 7 AI calls
🔤3,908tokens read and 1,170 written per plan (median)

At scale (estimate: 12 plans + 4 best-time lookups a month):

UsersAI cost per month
1 runner$0.013
100 runners$1.27
10,000 runners$127

Did cutting cost hurt quality? Removing the Navigator agent would have: with a stated preference it picked the best route 15 of 16 times, vs 6 of 16 for code alone. So it stays, and is skipped only when there's no note (code matched it 14 of 14 times), saving about $0.00012 per plan.

06 · Safety

Safe by design

  • 🛡️Hidden instructions in map data: a code Guard quarantines suspicious places before any agent sees them. 5 of 5 planted attacks caught.
  • 🧮No made-up facts: distances, safety, hours, hills and times come from code; agents that choose can only pick from lists.
  • ✋No surprise actions: email and calendar open as drafts you approve; event names never reach the AI.
  • 💰No runaway spending: 3 tries and 15,000 tokens per AI call, 20 plans an hour, $1 of AI a day. Tests show each one working.

08 · What's next

Where it goes from here

  • 📶Offline mode: guidance cut out on the trail without service. Save the map and turns to the phone first.
  • 🌐Put it online with accounts and saved routes; send routes to Strava and Garmin watches.
  • 🏃Run club sharing: one link, RSVPs, each runner's group plan on their phone.
  • 🗺️Better safety data: runner reports of dark stretches and risky crossings, plus city crash data.
